Legal

Terms & Conditions

1. Agreement & Scope

All works performed by Royal Construction Ltd. ("the Company") are governed by these Terms and Conditions. Engagement of services constitutes acceptance. Any changes to the agreed scope must be documented via a written Variation Order signed by both parties prior to commencement of additional works.

2. Payment Terms

  • Invoices due within 14 days of issue date
  • Late payments attract 2% monthly interest
  • A deposit of $1000 is required prior to mobilisation
  • Final payment due upon practical completion
  • Retention of 5% held for defects liability period

3. Warranties & Defects

  • 12-month defects liability period on all works
  • Structural warranty of 10 years on new builds
  • Defects reported in writing within liability period will be rectified at no charge
  • Warranty is void if alterations are made by unauthorised parties

4. Liability & Insurance

  • Public liability insurance: $10 million per occurrence
  • Workers' compensation insurance maintained for all staff
  • The Company's liability is limited to the contract value
  • Consequential or indirect losses are excluded
  • Client to maintain property insurance during works

5. Delays & Force Majeure

The Company shall not be held liable for delays resulting from events beyond reasonable control, including but not limited to: severe weather, acts of government, supply chain disruptions, industrial action, or pandemic-related restrictions. Extension of time will be granted accordingly in writing.

6. Dispute Resolution

  • Disputes must be raised in writing within 14 days of arising
  • Parties shall attempt good-faith negotiation for 30 days
  • Unresolved disputes referred to mediation or adjudication
  • Governing law: jurisdiction in which project is located
  • Legal costs awarded at arbitrator's discretion

Important Notice: These Terms and Conditions form part of every contract issued by Royal Construction Ltd. and should be read in conjunction with the specific project agreement. By signing any contract or letter of engagement with Royal Construction Ltd., the client acknowledges full acceptance of these terms. Royal Construction Ltd. reserves the right to update these terms with 30 days' written notice.
